How Robot 1 builds the roof

One part of the house, fully automated: from bare wall top plates to a finished, inspected roof. No one climbs a ladder or goes up on the roof. Robot 1 works from the ground, a remote supervisor approves its plan and its report, and the city inspector reviews that report.

The sequence

The robot

Robot 1: outdoor track loader with an autonomy kit
  • GPS, lidar and cameras on the roof rack; it sees the house, the material stacks and anyone nearby.
  • 3.5 m safety field: if a person walks in, it stops until they leave.
  • Telescoping 6 m boom with a small arm and a tool changer at the tip, so it reaches the 4.1 m ridge without leaving the ground.
  • Roof tools on its rack: scan head, vacuum lifter, nail gun, roll dispenser, shingle head, gutter roll-former.
  • Materials are delivered and staged on the lot before it starts; it picks each piece from the stack.
