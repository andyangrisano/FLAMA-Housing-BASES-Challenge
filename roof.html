A robotic factory line for the roof

Modular homes are already built indoors in factories, but by people working at each station. Our concept keeps the factory and replaces the people at the stations with robots. The roof is the example: lumber goes in one end, and a finished, shingled, inspected roof section comes out the other, set on its house module and ready to ship.

Modular factory today
  • Crews at each station measure, cut, nail and shingle by hand.
  • Roof framing, sheathing and shingling done on ladders and scaffolds over the modules.
  • Speed and quality depend on available skilled labour.
Our robotic line
  • Each station is a robot cell, fed directly from the house's digital model.
  • The roof is built at a working height on a jig, never on top of a house.
  • Every station scans its own work before the roof moves on; people supervise from a control room.
